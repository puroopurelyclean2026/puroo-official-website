AIR FRESHENER

Fresh Spaces. Better Experience.

Where & When to Use

  • Living rooms, bedrooms, offices, workspaces, common areas and vehicles.
  • Use daily, twice daily in high-traffic areas, or as needed for unpleasant odors.
  • Ideal before guests, meetings and special occasions.

How to Use

  1. Keep bottle upright, about 15–20 cm away.
  2. For room freshening, spray toward the center of the room.
  3. Use short bursts and allow fragrance to disperse naturally.
  4. Keep the area well ventilated.

Important

  • Avoid direct spraying on delicate fabrics, electronics, open flames and polished surfaces.
  • Do not mix with other cleaning chemicals.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.

GLASS CLEANER

Streak-Free Shine. Naturally Clear.

Where & When to Use

  • Windows, mirrors, glass doors, partitions, shower glass, display cabinets and glass tabletops.
  • Use daily or as needed; especially after rain, fingerprints or visible smudges.

How to Use

  1. Hold bottle upright and aim nozzle at the surface.
  2. Spray from about 15–20 cm away.
  3. Wipe with a clean dry microfiber cloth.
  4. Buff gently for extra shine.

Important

  • Do not use on LCD/LED screens or hot surfaces.
  • Avoid cleaning under strong direct sunlight.
  • Test tinted or coated glass in a small area first.

ORGANIC ALL PURPOSE CLEANER

One Cleaner. Many Surfaces.

Where & When to Use

  • Kitchen counters, sinks, bathroom surfaces, sealed floors, doors, handles, office desks and compatible furniture.
  • Use every day, after meals, after activities, and before or after guests.

How to Use

  1. Spray directly from about 15–20 cm away.
  2. Wipe with a clean cloth, sponge or microfiber.
  3. Rinse surfaces that may directly contact food.
  4. Allow other compatible surfaces to air dry.

Important

  • Avoid untreated wood, marble, natural stone, brass, copper and electronics.
  • Do not mix with bleach, ammonia or other chemicals.
  • Test delicate surfaces first.

ORGANIC DETERGENT POWDER

Powerful Clean. Gentle on Fabrics.

Where & When to Use

  • Top-load and front-load washing machines, hand washing, everyday family laundry and commercial laundry.
  • Suitable for many white, colored and dark fabrics; check garment care labels.

How to Use

  1. Measure the correct amount based on load size and soil level.
  2. For machine wash, add measured powder to the drum or dispenser.
  3. For hand wash, dissolve powder in water before washing.
  4. Rinse clothes thoroughly and dry normally.

Important

  • Do not use on wool, silk, leather, suede or fur unless the garment label permits it.
  • Do not apply powder directly onto dry fabric stains.
  • Keep the pack dry and tightly sealed.

ORGANIC DISINFECTANT

Clean High-Touch Areas Responsibly.

Where & When to Use

  • Compatible high-touch surfaces, bathrooms, kitchen counters, offices, classrooms, public areas and vehicle interiors.
  • Use daily, after cleaning, after meals in kitchen areas, or as needed in busy spaces.

How to Use

  1. Clean visible dirt or grease first.
  2. Spray the compatible surface evenly.
  3. Allow the surface to remain wet for the contact time stated on the product label.
  4. Wipe if needed, then allow to dry.

Important

  • Do not spray on eyes, mouth, open wounds or food without proper rinsing.
  • Avoid natural stone and unsealed wood unless compatibility is confirmed.
  • Never mix with bleach, ammonia, vinegar or other cleaning chemicals.

For disinfection claims and contact times, always follow the final approved product label and applicable regulatory requirements.

ORGANIC LIQUID BLEACH

Deep Clean. Brighten. Use with Care.

Where & When to Use

  • Laundry whites/light colors and compatible hard surfaces such as selected floors, tiles, counters, tubs, sinks and drains.
  • Use as needed for cleaning, brightening and stain removal.

How to Use

  1. Wear appropriate protection and follow the dilution guide on the product label.
  2. Prepare only the amount needed.
  3. Apply to the compatible surface or laundry as directed.
  4. Rinse thoroughly where required, especially food-contact surfaces.

Important

  • Never mix bleach with ammonia, acids, vinegar or other cleaners.
  • Avoid wool, silk, leather, natural stone, aluminum and delicate finishes.
  • Store in the original labeled container away from heat and direct sunlight.

SCENTED HAND SOAP

Gentle on Hands. Ready to Use.

When to Use

  • Before meals or food handling.
  • After using the restroom, coming from outside or touching frequently handled public surfaces.
  • After handling pets and after coughing, sneezing or blowing your nose.

How to Use

  1. Wet hands and pump a small amount of soap.
  2. Rub palms, backs of hands, between fingers, thumbs, fingernails and wrists.
  3. Wash for at least 20 seconds.
  4. Rinse with clean water and dry thoroughly.

Important

  • For external use only. Avoid eyes and open wounds.
  • Do not ingest.
  • Keep the pump clean and store in a cool, dry place.

ORGANIC TOILET CLEANER

Powerful Clean. Fresh Bathroom.

Where & When to Use

  • Toilet bowls, urinals, bathroom sinks, tiles and compatible porcelain or ceramic fixtures.
  • Use every day, twice daily in high-traffic restrooms, or as needed for stains, limescale and odor.

How to Use

  1. Flush to remove excess surface water.
  2. Apply inside the bowl, under the rim and to the sides.
  3. Allow to sit briefly as directed on the label.
  4. Scrub if needed, then flush or rinse thoroughly.

Important

  • Avoid marble, limestone, terrazzo and other acid-sensitive natural stone.
  • Do not mix with bleach or other cleaning chemicals.
  • Do not use on cracked or damaged porcelain.
